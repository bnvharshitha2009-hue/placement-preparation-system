Placement Preparation & Skill Gap Analysis System

Find out where you stand, plan what to study, and track your progress.

Campus placements test aptitude, coding, technical knowledge and communication. This system is designed to help students see which of these skills are strong and which need work (the skill gap), turn those gaps into a study plan, and follow their improvement over time. The project is built in three modules, one for each team member.
